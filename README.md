# Awesome-Farm-Management-Software

## Top Farm Management Software Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Field Records, Crop Planning, Inventory, Livestock, Agronomy & Farm Business Operations*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Farm Management Software**. These systems help growers and agribusinesses record field activities, plan crops, track inventory and inputs, manage livestock, analyze agronomy data, and run the farm as a business.



**Examples** include Granular, Trimble Ag Software, Agworld, FarmERP, Croptracker, Conservis, EOS Crop Monitoring, AgriWebb, SourceTrace, and Farmbrite (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Commercial farm-management platforms dominate mid-to-large operations. The strongest open alternative is **farmOS**—a mature, community-driven web application for farm record keeping. This section expands farmOS and related open tools and remains realistic about commercial feature depth.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Granular](https://www.granular.ag/)**  

  Farm business management software (Corteva) focused on field profitability, operational records, and agronomic analysis for row-crop operations.



- **[Trimble Ag Software / PTx FarmENGAGE](https://agriculture.trimble.com/)**  

  Precision agriculture and farm operations platform for field records, machinery data, task management, and multi-brand equipment integration.



- **[Agworld](https://www.agworld.com/)**  

  Collaborative cloud farm-management platform connecting growers, agronomists, consultants, and retailers around standardized field and crop records.



- **[FarmERP](https://www.farmerp.com/)**  

  Enterprise farm and agribusiness management software covering operations, supply chain, and financial processes for larger farming organizations.



- **[Croptracker](https://www.croptracker.com/)**  

  Farm management and traceability software focused on harvest, labor, and compliance records for specialty crop producers.



- **[Conservis](https://www.conservis.ag/)**  

  Independent farm management platform strong on inventory, contracts, grain tracking, and operational oversight for diversified row-crop farms.



- **[EOS Crop Monitoring](https://eos.com/)**  

  Satellite-based crop monitoring and analytics platform providing vegetation indices, field insights, and remote sensing support for growers.



- **[AgriWebb](https://www.agriwebb.com/)**  

  Livestock and pasture management platform popular with cattle and sheep operations for animal records, grazing, and compliance.



- **[SourceTrace](https://www.sourcetrace.com/)**  

  Digital agriculture and farm management platform focused on smallholder and supply-chain traceability use cases.



- **[Farmbrite](https://www.farmbrite.com/)**  

  All-in-one farm management software for smaller and diversified farms covering livestock, crops, inventory, and financial tracking.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[farmOS](https://github.com/farmOS/farmOS)**  

  Mature, community-driven open-source web application for farm management, planning, and record keeping—assets, logs, areas, sensors, crops, livestock, and mapping (Drupal-based, GPL).



- **[farmOS Field Kit and mobile companions](https://github.com/farmOS)**  

  Offline-first and mobile companion apps that extend farmOS for in-field data capture.



- **[farmOS Aggregator and multi-farm tools](https://github.com/farmOS/farmOS-aggregator)**  

  Open microservice for interacting with and aggregating data from multiple farmOS instances.



- **[farmOS.py and API client libraries](https://github.com/farmOS/farmOS.py)**  

  Python and other language clients for integrating external systems with farmOS.



- **[Open precision-ag and sensor open collectors](https://github.com/)**  

  Community firmware and backends for logging weather, soil, and equipment data into farm records.



- **[Mapping and GIS open stacks for agriculture](https://github.com/)**  

  QGIS, PostGIS, and related tools commonly used alongside farmOS for field boundaries and spatial analysis.



- **[Crop and livestock open record templates](https://github.com/)**  

  Structured data models and spreadsheets that can seed open or commercial farm systems.



- **[Inventory and input open trackers](https://github.com/)**  

  Simple open tools for seed, chemical, and feed inventory that integrate with broader farm records.



- **[Traceability and lot open systems](https://github.com/)**  

  Community projects focused on batch and harvest traceability for food-safety and market requirements.



- **[Documentation and farmOS open playbooks](https://farmos.org/)**  

  Official and community guides for installing, hosting, and extending farmOS.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Self-hosting **farmOS** for full control of farm records, maps, and logs—especially attractive for privacy-conscious or research-oriented operations.

- Combining farmOS with open GIS and sensor stacks for a complete digital farm record system.

- Accepting that large-scale financial planning, multi-farm enterprise reporting, deep equipment telematics, and commercial agronomy networks still favor platforms such as Granular, Trimble, Agworld, Conservis, FarmERP, and AgriWebb.

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, offline capability, and extensibility for independent growers and research farms.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Record assets, areas, and logs in farmOS → capture field data via Field Kit or mobile forms → integrate sensors and maps with open GIS tools → optionally export to commercial platforms for advanced analytics or supply-chain requirements. Suitable for individual farms, cooperatives, and research projects. Larger commercial operations often combine or migrate to commercial farm-management suites.
